# Frequently Asked Questions

Compiled from recurring customer support tickets — updated quarterly by the CS team.


## Q1. How long does delivery take?

It depends on your city. Karachi/Lahore/Islamabad customers usually get their order in 2-3 days. Other
cities can take 4-7 days, and remote areas like Gilgit-Baltistan can take up to two weeks. See the full
Shipping Policy document for the exact table.


## Q2. Can I pay online instead of COD?

Yes — we accept JazzCash, Easypaisa, and all major debit/credit cards at checkout. Prepaid orders are
generally dispatched slightly faster since they skip the COD confirmation step.


## What if I miss the courier's call?

The rider will usually attempt delivery up to 3 times (though in practice this varies a lot by area — some
couriers in smaller cities only try once, unfortunately). If you miss all attempts the order gets sent back to
us and marked RTO.


## Q3. Do you offer free returns?

Only for damaged, defective, or wrong items — see Section 4 of the Refund Policy. For regular size/fit
exchanges, the first exchange per order is free; a second one has a small Rs. 150 charge.


## Q4. My tracking says 'delivered' but I never got the package. What now?

This is treated as a priority escalation, not a normal delivery question — please contact support directly
with your order number rather than waiting for the chatbot; this scenario is not something the automated
flow should resolve on its own.


## Q5. Can I return sale items?

Items discounted more than 30% are exchange-only (no cash refund) and must be exchanged within 7
days. Regular-priced items follow the normal 14-day policy.


## Is customization/stitching returnable?

No — once an item is cut/stitched to your measurements it can't be resold, so these are final sale. This is
one of the most common escalation-avoidable questions, so it's worth checking with the customer
BEFORE they order if their size is unusual.


## Q6. I want to cancel my order, how do I do that?

If the order hasn't been dispatched yet, just reply 'cancel' to the confirmation message and it'll be cancelled
automatically. Once dispatched, it can't be cancelled mid-transit, but you can refuse delivery and it'll be
treated as a standard RTO with no charge to you.


## Q7. Do remote areas pay extra for COD?

Yes, there's a remote-area surcharge (Rs. 200-350 depending on exact location) that's added at checkout
automatically for Gilgit-Baltistan, Chitral, and similar remote destinations — this isn't a delivery fee, it's
separate and shown as a line item.

Note: this FAQ is a quick-reference summary maintained informally by the CS team and may lag behind the formal Shipping and
Refund Policy documents after a policy change — where the two disagree, the formal policy document is authoritative.

## 1. General Eligibility

Items purchased from UrbanThread are eligible for return or exchange within 14 calendar days of the
delivery date shown on the courier's proof-of-delivery record, not the order date. The item must be unworn,
unwashed, and in its original packaging with all tags attached. UrbanThread reserves the right to refuse a
return that shows visible signs of wear, odor (including perfume or deodorant residue), or alteration. This
policy applies uniformly across COD and prepaid orders; payment method does not affect return eligibility.


### CATEGORY-SPECIFIC EXCEPTIONS

Certain product categories carry tighter or different rules than the general policy above, primarily for
hygiene, customization, or promotional reasons. Agents must check the category table below before
confirming eligibility to a customer, as several categories are non-refundable outright.

Category: Innerwear & lingerie | Returnable?: No | Window: — | Notes: Hygiene exception, no exceptions made
Category: Footwear | Returnable?: Yes | Window: 7 days | Notes: Shorter window; box must be undamaged
Category: Accessories & jewelry | Returnable?: Yes | Window: 14 days | Notes: Original packaging mandatory
Category: Sale / clearance items (>30% off) | Returnable?: Exchange only | Window: 7 days | Notes: No cash refund, store credit or exchange

## 2. Size / Fit Exchanges

Size exchanges are treated distinctly from returns and are UrbanThread's most common post-delivery
customer contact reason, particularly on unstitched and semi-formal lines where size charts are
self-measured by the customer. A size exchange request does not require the original packaging to be in
perfect condition, provided the garment itself is unworn and unwashed. One free size exchange is
permitted per order; a second exchange on the same order is subject to a Rs. 150 handling and
reverse-shipping charge, deducted from any refund or charged to the customer for a cash-on-delivery
exchange shipment.


#### Handling hesitant cancellation requests

A customer message such as "cancel kar do, size sahi nahi lagega" is a sizing concern, not a firm
cancellation, and should be treated per this section rather than processed as an outright order
cancellation. Agents should offer the size chart and this exchange policy before processing any
cancellation on sizing grounds. Repeated hesitation after this offer should be escalated per the standard
low-confidence escalation path rather than resolved unilaterally by an automated agent.


## 3. Refund Method and Timeline

For prepaid orders, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of the
returned item passing warehouse quality inspection. For COD orders, since no original payment method
exists, refunds are issued via bank transfer or JazzCash/Easypaisa to an account nominated by the
customer, which typically adds 2-3 additional business days for account verification. Store credit, where
chosen instead of a cash refund, is applied instantly upon return approval and does not require the
verification step.


### 4. DAMAGED OR INCORRECT ITEMS

Items that arrive damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered are handled outside the standard
14-day return window described in Section 1 above and are not subject to the category exceptions in the
table (i.e. even a stitched or innerwear item that arrives genuinely damaged is eligible for return on this
basis). The customer must report the issue within 48 hours of delivery and provide a photo of the item;
UrbanThread bears the full cost of the reverse shipment in these cases, and no restocking or handling fee
applies regardless of category.

This section was reworded in v2.1 (see revision history) following a legal review that clarified that the
48-hour reporting window is a target for prompt reporting, not a hard cutoff that forfeits the customer's
statutory consumer-protection rights; agents should not refuse a damaged-item claim solely for being
reported on day 3 or 4 if the item is clearly defective on inspection.

## 1. Scope

This document governs the shipping timelines, courier handling procedures, and cash-on-delivery (COD)
confirmation process for all orders processed through UrbanThread's e-commerce channels. It
supersedes any verbal commitments made by customer support representatives and should be treated as
the single source of truth for shipping-related customer queries. Where a conflict exists between this
document and information published on the storefront FAQ page, this document governs unless the
storefront has been updated after the version date shown in the revision history above.


## 2. Order Processing Timeline

Orders placed before 3:00 PM PKT on a business day are dispatched the same day, subject to inventory
availability at the fulfilling warehouse. Orders placed after 3:00 PM PKT, or on Saturday after 1:00 PM,
Sunday, or a designated public holiday, are queued for dispatch on the following business day. During
high-volume periods (Eid, 11.11, Black Friday, year-end sale), dispatch may be delayed by up to two
additional business days; customers should be informed of this proactively where an agent is aware a sale
period is active.

## 3. Cash-on-Delivery (COD) Confirmation Process

Approximately 88% of UrbanThread's order volume is COD. Because COD orders carry a materially
higher return-to-origin (RTO) risk than prepaid orders, every COD order above Rs. 1,500 must go through
an automated or manual confirmation step before being handed to the courier. This is separate from, and
in addition to, the courier's own delivery-attempt call.


#### 3a. What counts as a valid confirmation

A customer reply of "yes", "haan", "confirm", "theek hai", "ok bhej dein" or equivalent affirmative language,
sent within 24 hours of the confirmation message, counts as valid confirmation. A reply asking a clarifying
question (e.g. about delivery timing, price, or size) does NOT count as confirmation or as cancellation — it
should be answered and confirmation re-requested.


#### 3b. Non-response handling

If no response is received within 24 hours, a single automated reminder is sent. If there is still no response
after a further 24 hours, the order is flagged for manual outbound calling by the CS team before being
marked RTO-risk and held from dispatch. Orders should never be auto-cancelled purely for non-response
without at least one outbound call attempt, per the Q2 2025 ops review.

## 5. Failed Delivery Attempts

Couriers are contracted to make up to three delivery attempts before returning a parcel to origin. In
practice, Tier 3 and remote-area couriers frequently make only one attempt before marking a parcel
undeliverable and initiating return — this is a known gap in courier SLA compliance and is tracked
separately in the monthly courier performance review, not something the support agent can resolve in real
time. Where a customer reports zero delivery attempts despite a "delivery attempted" status on the
tracking page, this should be escalated to the logistics coordinator rather than promised a specific
resolution.


#### Address Correction Requests

A customer may request an address correction any time before the order status shows "Out for Delivery".
Once a parcel is out for delivery, the courier's own rider must be contacted directly; UrbanThread's system
cannot push a mid-route address change to most courier partners' apps in real time. PostEx is the sole
exception, supporting live address edits via their rider app.


## 6. Force Majeure and Regional Disruptions

UrbanThread shall not be held liable for shipping delays arising from circumstances beyond its reasonable
control, including but not limited to: political unrest, transport strikes, extreme weather events, internet or
telecom outages affecting order confirmation, government-mandated curfews, or courier partner-side
operational disruptions. In such cases, the standard delivery windows in Section 2 are suspended and a
revised estimate is communicated once available. This clause has been invoked most recently during the
November 2025 fog-related flight disruptions affecting air-freighted Tier-1-to-remote shipments.
