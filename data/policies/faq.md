# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How long will my order take to arrive?**
A: 2–3 business days for major cities (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad), 4–6 days for other cities/towns, and 6–8 days for remote/rural areas. See Shipping Policy for details.

**Q: Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?**
A: Yes, on all orders under Rs. 15,000. Orders above Rs. 15,000 require a partial advance payment to confirm.

**Q: Is there a delivery charge?**
A: Delivery is free on orders above Rs. 2,500. Orders below that have a flat Rs. 200 charge, with no extra fee for choosing COD.

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: A tracking link is sent via WhatsApp once your order is dispatched. You can also ask for your order status directly in the WhatsApp chat anytime.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after ordering?**
A: Yes, anytime before dispatch — just reply in the WhatsApp conversation. Once dispatched, the address can't be changed, but delivery can be rescheduled.

**Q: What if I miss the rider's delivery attempt?**
A: Up to 2 redelivery attempts are made. If both are missed, the order is returned to origin.

**Q: My order hasn't arrived within the promised time — what now?**
A: Occasional delays happen due to weather, courier disruptions, or holidays. You'll be notified proactively via WhatsApp if a delay goes beyond 2 extra business days. If you haven't heard from us, message us your order details.

**Q: Can I return an item if I don't like it?**
A: Yes, most items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging. Electronics have a shorter 3-day window and must be unopened. See Refund & Return Policy for exceptions.

**Q: I received a damaged or wrong item — what do I do?**
A: Message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours with photos/video. There's no return charge in this case, and we'll offer a replacement or full refund.

**Q: How will I get my refund?**
A: Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. COD orders are refunded via bank transfer or JazzCash/Easypaisa in the same timeframe, after the item is inspected.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**
A: Yes, free of charge anytime before dispatch via WhatsApp. Once dispatched, it can't be cancelled — you can refuse it at delivery or return it afterward.
