# Refund & Return Policy

## Return Window
- Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for most items
- Electronics and appliances: 3 days from delivery, and must be unopened/unused with original packaging and seal intact
- Clothing and accessories: 7 days, tags must still be attached and items unworn
- Perishable, personal care, and made-to-order/customized items are non-returnable

## Conditions for a Valid Return
- Item must be unused, undamaged, and in its original packaging
- Original invoice or order confirmation (WhatsApp order message is accepted as proof)
- Free gifts or bundled items received with the order must be returned along with the main product

## Damaged or Wrong Item Received
- Customers must report a damaged, defective, or incorrect item within 48 hours of delivery, ideally with photos/video shared over WhatsApp
- No return shipping charge applies when the fault is on our side (damaged, defective, or wrong item)
- Replacement is offered first where the item is in stock; otherwise a full refund is issued

## Refund Method & Timeline
- For prepaid orders: refund is issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected
- For COD orders: refund is issued via bank transfer or JazzCash/Easypaisa within 5–7 business days after inspection, since there is no original payment method to reverse to
- Advance payment made on orders above Rs. 15,000 (see Shipping Policy) is refunded the same way if the order is cancelled or returned

## Return Pickup
- A rider is scheduled for pickup once a return request is confirmed over WhatsApp
- If pickup is missed twice, the customer is asked to self-ship, with courier charges reimbursed on confirmation of a valid return

## Cancellations
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge any time before dispatch via the WhatsApp conversation
- Once dispatched, an order cannot be cancelled — it can only be refused at delivery or returned per this policy after receipt
