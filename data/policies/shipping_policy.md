# Shipping Policy

## Delivery Timelines
- Major cities (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad): 2–3 business days
- Other cities and towns: 4–6 business days
- Remote/rural areas: 6–8 business days
- Orders placed after 6 PM are processed the next business day

## Cash on Delivery (COD)
- COD is available on all orders under Rs. 15,000
- Orders above Rs. 15,000 require partial advance payment to confirm
- Riders may call before delivery to confirm availability — customers should keep their phone reachable
- If a customer is unavailable, up to 2 redelivery attempts are made before the order is returned to origin

## Shipping Charges
- Free delivery on orders above Rs. 2,500
- Flat Rs. 200 delivery charge on orders below Rs. 2,500
- No additional charge for COD

## Delays
- Delays beyond the stated window can happen due to weather, courier disruptions, or public holidays
- Customers are notified proactively via WhatsApp if a delay exceeds 2 extra business days

## Order Tracking
- A tracking link is sent via WhatsApp once the order is dispatched
- Customers can also request their status directly in the WhatsApp chat at any time

## Address Changes
- Address can be changed any time before dispatch by replying in the WhatsApp conversation
- Once dispatched, address changes are not possible — only rescheduling delivery is possible
