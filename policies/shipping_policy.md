# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## 1. Shipping Options & Timeframes
* **Standard Shipping:** Takes 3-5 business days. Cost: $4.99 (Free on orders over $50).
* **Expedited Shipping:** Takes 2 business days. Cost: $12.99.
* **Overnight Shipping:** Next business day delivery. Cost: $24.99.

## 2. Order Processing Time
* Orders placed before 2 PM EST are processed the same business day.
* Orders placed after 2 PM EST are processed the next business day.
* We do not process or ship orders on weekends or public holidays.

## 3. Delayed Shipments
* If a package is delayed by the carrier (e.g., FedEx, UPS) and misses the estimated delivery date, we will refund the shipping cost.
* If a package is delayed for more than 7 days past the estimated delivery date, the customer can request a full refund or a replacement.

## 4. Lost Packages
* A package is officially considered "Lost" if there has been no tracking update for 5 consecutive days.
* In the event of a lost package, we will immediately issue a full refund or ship a replacement via expedited shipping at no extra cost.

## 5. Incorrect Address
* If a customer provides an incorrect shipping address and the package is returned to us, the customer must pay a $5.99 reshipping fee.
